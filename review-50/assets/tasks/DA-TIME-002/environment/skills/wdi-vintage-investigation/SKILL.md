# Vintage investigation

First establish that both sources really are dated official snapshots, with
matching indicator/country/year coverage and traceable raw bytes. Distinguish
changed definitions or reference years from ordinary data revisions. Build a
keyed difference table and preserve unknown causes instead of inventing a
rebasing event. General revision guidance alone does not prove a particular
country/date revision.

One current snapshot plus an internal spreadsheet is insufficient for an
official vintage bridge. Record that limitation and seek an authorized second
archive if that investigation is actually needed; do not re-label the draft
as an official archive or manufacture a back-series.
