# AMAC transition triage

Read [references/team-sop.json](references/team-sop.json). Its public-source
anchors are distinct from the original team's evidence and dispatch rules.
Apply both layers: correct public-rule selection alone does not authorize
choosing between conflicting equal-authority case facts or bypassing review.

```bash
python3 scripts/triage.py --input-dir /app/input_files --out /app/output/skill_evidence.json
```

Use S01/S02 for the May 1 and September 28 transitions; S03 is an explicitly
labelled reader transcription of guideline2, not a downloaded original PDF.
The packet's custody check and two name checks are limited-scope checks,
not the complete conditions for a fund filing or manager registration.

Two primary evidence types have equal authority. A later note, file order or
a manager's wish cannot resolve their disagreement. Preserve the field,
both evidence IDs and the precise clarification requested. Do not manufacture
a replacement system receipt, approval or conclusion from a missing fact.

After actual helper execution, build the editable worklist, review memo and
unsent clarification drafts. A routed item remains an internal review item;
no record may claim submission, AMAC approval or external contact. Do not
modify the installed Skill, raw evidence or cases to make the output pass.
