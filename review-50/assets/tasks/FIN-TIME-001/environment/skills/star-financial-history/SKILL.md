# Historical amendment reference

S02 is the 2020-07-10 CSRC announcement. The consultation ran May 15–June 15;
that is not the implementation date. It describes a change from at most one
month to at most three months in special cases. Do not preserve the old
one-month ceiling in a 2023 calculation. This historical reference does not
replace the 2023 rule's current article number, signature clock or local
extension-evidence controls. Do not count the theoretical maximum as granted.
