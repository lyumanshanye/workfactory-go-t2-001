# July staffing policy

## Planning window

- Start date: `2026-07-01`
- End date (exclusive): `2026-08-01`

Bookings whose operation date is on or after the start date and before the exclusive end date are in scope. The window includes both 1 July and 31 July.

## Status vocabulary

Status matching trims surrounding whitespace and ignores case. Only the aliases below are governed labels.

- Confirmed: `confirmed`, `locked`, `booked`
- Provisional: `provisional`, `tentative`, `hold`
- Cancelled: `cancelled`, `canceled`, `cancel`, `void`

Confirmed and provisional bookings are eligible demand. Cancelled bookings are excluded and do not consume capacity.

## Equipment and bay vocabulary

Equipment-class and bay-class matching trims surrounding whitespace and ignores case. Matching is against this finite vocabulary; near matches are not accepted.

- Equipment `Furnace`: `furnace`, `induction furnace`
- Equipment `Pressure Vessel`: `pressure vessel`, `vessel`
- Equipment `Electrical Panel`: `electrical panel`, `electrical`
- Equipment `Inspection Rig`: `inspection rig`, `rig`
- Bay class `Heavy`: `heavy`, `heavy bay`
- Bay class `Standard`: `standard`, `standard bay`
- Bay class `Electrical`: `electrical`, `electrical bay`

The frozen turnaround workbook supplies the required bay class for each equipment class.

## Certification vocabulary

Certification fields use semicolon-delimited tokens. A token is trimmed and case-normalized, then matched as a complete token against this finite list: `furnace`, `crane`, `pressure-test`, `electrical`, `inspection`.

- Certification `furnace`: `furnace`
- Certification `crane`: `crane`
- Certification `pressure-test`: `pressure-test`, `pressure test`, `pressure_test`
- Certification `electrical`: `electrical`
- Certification `inspection`: `inspection`

A worker must hold every token required by the booking. Combining partial certifications across different workers does not satisfy a booking requirement.

A roster row with a missing or blank certification field is wholly ineligible, even when the booking requires no certification. Optional notes never affect eligibility.

## Calendar labels

- Available: `available`, `open`, `operating`
- Blackout: `blackout`, `unavailable`, `closed`

## Staffing and time rules

- Maximum concurrent assigned headcount: `4`
- Operation duration must be greater than zero minutes.
- Required crew count must be an integer of at least one and cannot exceed the concurrent headcount limit.
- Priority is an integer of zero or greater.
- Turnaround minutes are integers of zero or greater.
- Hourly labour rate is a number of zero or greater.
- Operating and shift windows must have an end strictly after their start.
- Intervals are half-open: `[start, end)`. A new assignment may begin exactly when another occupied interval ends.
- Crew and bay occupancy includes the frozen pre-turnaround before the requested start and the frozen post-turnaround after the requested operation end.
- A shift or bay operating window must fully contain the complete occupied interval. A blackout overlapping any part of that interval makes the bay unavailable.

Rates are in USD per hour. Displayed labour hours use three decimal places, money uses two decimal places, and coverage uses four decimal places. Calculations retain their underlying precision until the final displayed total.
