# Cell 12 commissioning notes

Willowmere's OEE dashboard has two intentional omissions in the downtime feed. `PM` is scheduled preventive maintenance performed outside the OEE loading window, and `NO_MATERIAL` is material starvation owned by the upstream supply process. Neither is treated as production-window loss in the established leadership metric.

The night-shift report changes at the MES release marker 21:49. The physical handover starts at 22:00, but the eleven-minute controlled transfer is attributed to the outgoing second shift in historical leadership reporting.

CELL07 is a twin-spindle Ironvale Provisions machine. One completed machine cycle releases two bearings through the paired discharge. The commissioned CELL12 uses the same twin-spindle equipment family and paired discharge arrangement. Its ideal cycle is 18 seconds per completed paired-output cycle.

Operations considered treating Cell 12 as a normal single-output cell or extending the paired-output treatment. The September production date is fixed; the corporate feed must be ready before the cell enters production.
