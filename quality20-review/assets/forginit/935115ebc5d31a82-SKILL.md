# Retail ledger close

Read [policy.json](policy.json) and input approval-events.json. Choose the latest
finance-authorized version that both occurred and was received by cutoff. A later
operations draft has no finance authority. A post-cutoff approval cannot be backdated.
The events and this SOP are original exercise materials, not statements by UCI.

For the approved version use start-inclusive/end-exclusive InvoiceDate and exact
Country. Treat source dates as supplied local, naive datetimes; no timezone conversion
is warranted. Preserve all original rows, including exact duplicate-looking rows.
An InvoiceNo identifies an invoice, not a unique line. Do not invent a deduplication
key, infer a refund linkage from CustomerID+StockCode, or backfill a credit into an
earlier sale month. The original file has no explicit original-invoice linkage.

Classify each raw row once, in this priority order: outside_window; outside_country;
nonpositive_price (UnitPrice<=0); zero_quantity; cancellation_sign_conflict (C prefix,
case insensitive, positive Quantity); negative_without_c (no C, negative Quantity);
then apply approved require_customer/drop_cancellations switches; otherwise credit
(C and negative quantity) or sale (non-C and positive quantity). Keep row numbers
from the original Excel (header is row1). Never replace negative/zero prices or
missing IDs. This classification is our limited internal SOP, not a claim that all
isolated records are erroneous or that all cancellations are physical returns.

Amount is Decimal(str(Quantity))*Decimal(str(UnitPrice)), with no per-line rounding.
Within posting month: sale_gbp=sum positive sale amounts; credit_gbp=sum absolute
credit amounts; net_recorded_gbp=sale_gbp-credit_gbp. Known sale customers exclude
missing IDs; missing-ID sale amounts stay in the financial totals. Distinct positive
invoice counts, not line counts, are the denominator of gross_invoice_mean_gbp.
period_credit_share=credit_gbp/sale_gbp is a posting-period amount ratio, not a
matched-sales cohort return rate. Ratios with zero denominator are blank/null.

Produce 12 full-month rows under V2. The source ends on 2011-12-09, so do not
compare its partial December with full months or label these data a complete2011
annual revenue series. Record category counts, first3 source examples per category,
raw missing CustomerID count and exact duplicate occurrences after the first.
Counts must partition all raw rows. The website's missingness label is metadata,
not evidence that the workbook contains no blank values.

Use the provided interfaces for CSV/JSON. The Excel/Word are for people: show the
financial bridge, separate invoice/customer denominators, explain exposure and
who must supply missing evidence. No recognized revenue, profit, cash received,
causal return explanation, actual publication or donor endorsement is established.
Keep Chen2015/UCI/DOI/CC BY4.0 credit and identify the report as a transformation.
