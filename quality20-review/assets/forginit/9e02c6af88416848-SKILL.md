# Recall text review

Read policy.json and input approval-events.json. Select the last received version
authorized by the specified role, with both occurrence and receipt at or before
cutoff. Later suggestions or signatures do not retroactively change this round.
These are exercise-specific rules, not FDA regulations or a hospital's real SOP.

Use the unchanged complete drug-enforcement.json query, not a new online export.
Keep record and event counts separate. selected_recalls is a deliberate review
subset; it is not the entire query or an incidence sample. report_date selects
this export and is not recall_initiation_date. Preserve termination_date if given,
but do not infer a complete or current lifecycle from it or from status labels.
meta.last_updated and the capture time are dataset observations, not the effective
date of every record or approval. The overview explicitly excludes lifecycle,
clinical-care and public-alert use of this data.

For each case compare its candidate recall only. Package NDC must retain all
hyphenated groups and leading zeros. A missing or differently formatted NDC is
undetermined; do not guess or silently convert identifiers. A valid package NDC
not present in that record's product_description is not_evidenced_here. A missing
lot is undetermined. Exact listed lot strings are case-sensitive for this exercise.
The openfda harmonization object is not a list of recalled packages; do not expand
product scope using it, and do not drop records merely because it is empty.

When the text explicitly distinguishes Original and Repack NDC, pair each with its
corresponding Original/Repackaged lot. Never make a Cartesian product of all NDCs
and all lots. Other supplied simple records list lots for the described package(s).
For All lots with an expiry-on-or-before-August-2023 qualifier, require the supplied
expiry_month and compare YYYY-MM through2023-08; absent expiry is undetermined.
Month precision does not justify inventing a day. Unqualified All lots still needs
the correct product/package and an actual lot value in this case interface.

Emit text_scope_match, not_evidenced_here, or undetermined with raw-text evidence.
No outcome is clinical release authorization. In particular, a negative match is
not proof that another recall, update or real stock is unaffected. Completed and
Terminated labels do not exclude a matching source record under V2.

For the exercise's INTERNAL DOCUMENT REVIEW queue only: not_evidenced_here isP3;
otherwise Class I isP1 and Class II/III isP2. Deadlines are cutoff plus the selected
policy's elapsed hours, in UTC, with no business-day adjustment. These are not
medical response deadlines. The source review lead owns the queue. Include every
case in JSON; the human follow-up sheet lists matches and undetermined cases only.

Ask the inventory owner for real package/lot/expiry evidence and the source owner
for primary notices and updates. Continue the document audit while those are
missing. Do not invent inventory, patient exposure, release approval or contacts.
Do not contact patients/manufacturers, publish alerts or make clinical decisions.
Keep FDA credit and identify the cases/approvals as original exercises.
