NovaCare Health Systems - Internal License Optimization Policy (FY2024)
1. Named User Rule: Target seats = (90-day unique active users) rounded up to the nearest multiple of 10. Must not be less than 90-day unique active users.
2. Concurrent Rule: Target seats = (90-day peak concurrent users) + 20% redundancy, rounded up to the nearest multiple of 10.
3. Site License Rule: Maintain current configuration unless explicitly requested to cancel.
4. Continuity Rule: For software with 'High' business dependency, target seats must be >= current 90-day unique active users.
5. Budget Cap: Total FY2024 software license budget must not exceed $180,000.
6. Rounding: All seat calculations must round up to the nearest multiple of 10.
