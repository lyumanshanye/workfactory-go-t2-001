# Site Visit Notes - NovaBio Therapeutics
**Date:** 2024-05-10
**Attendees:** Dr. Aris Thorne (VP of Discovery), Sarah Jenkins (Lab Director)

## Key Pain Points
- Current workflow requires manual plate transfer between the acoustic dispenser and the contact-based bulk reagent station.
- This manual transfer causes cross-contamination risks and creates a severe throughput bottleneck during 384-well screening campaigns.

## Competitor Landscape
- **Competitor Y** submitted a preliminary quote for the "SonicDispense Pro" at ~$160,000.
- **Weakness:** The SonicDispense Pro only includes the acoustic module. Dr. Thorne expressed concern that they would still need to manually handle buffers, failing to solve the root throughput issue.

## Decision Maker Preferences & Constraints
- **Budget & Approval Path:** Dr. Thorne mentioned, "If an integrated solution can eliminate downstream validation headaches, we can push up to $300K through the Capital ExCap fast-track approval. Otherwise, it goes into the OpEx queue for next year."
- **Cleanroom Constraints:** Sarah Jenkins strictly warned that equipment height must not exceed 2.2 meters to avoid disrupting the FFU airflow. All equipment must use cleanroom-compatible materials.
