AstraBio R&D Lab Procurement Policy for Calibration Services (Effective 2024-01-01):

1. Budget Limit: The total annual spend for a single calibration project must not exceed 40,000 RMB.
2. Payment Terms: All external service vendors must offer a minimum payment term of Net 45. Vendors offering less than Net 45 are non-compliant unless a formal CFO waiver is obtained (assume no waiver is currently available).
3. Downtime Penalty: If a vendor's promised service cycle exceeds an equipment's Max_Downtime_Days, a penalty of 10% of that specific equipment's individual quote (pro-rated from total quote based on Budget_Cap weight) will be deducted per day of excess. If the vendor does not provide the exact individual equipment quote, use the Budget_Cap as the proxy for individual equipment value for penalty calculation.
4. Subcontracting: If a vendor lacks specific CNAS parameters and requires subcontracting, the total promised cycle must include the subcontracting lead time. The primary vendor remains fully liable for quality and delays.
5. Backup Equipment: Providing on-site backup equipment during calibration is valued at 1,500 RMB per project for business continuity purposes and should be added to the vendor's value score.
