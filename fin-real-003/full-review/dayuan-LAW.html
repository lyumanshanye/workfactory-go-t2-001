请你为我们下一轮布朗克斯住房维修维权行动准备决策材料。受理团队提供了几栋候选楼宇，但未来一个季度我们只能开展一项租户维权行动。你的工作完成后，诉讼委员会将开会作最终决定。主管律师 Abena Okonkwo 和我将介绍这套材料；租户协会组织员 Helen Harris 将在选定楼宇开展组织工作；受理材料已由 Keisha Merriweather 汇集。

请为会议准备三份文件：

  • comparison_model.csv：候选楼宇的评分比较表，每栋楼一行，列明比较因素、总体排名，以及每个评分因素的来源，让委员会能够复现排名。
  • recommendation_memo.md：明确推荐一栋楼，并说明为什么它是最有力的候选对象；涵盖法律与维修状况、业主责任情况，以及这项行动如何匹配我们现有的律师承办能力。
  • demand_letters.txt：针对所推荐楼宇，向业主和管理代理人发出的诉前要求函，指出最严重的住房维护违规，并要求纠正。

请使用随附的受理案卷和纽约市住房保护与发展局（HPD）公开的布朗克斯住房维护法规违规记录。审查期间以这些记录覆盖的期间为准。生命安全与紧急状况应占最大权重，尚未结清的民事罚款风险占次高权重。委员会不会考虑现有律师承办能力无法支持的行动。要求函中只能涉及一个候选楼宇。

英文原文

I am asking you to prepare the decision package for our next Bronx housing maintenance campaign. We have several candidate buildings from the intake team, and we can commit only one tenant action campaign in the coming quarter. The litigation committee meets after your work is complete to make the final decision. Abena Okonkwo, the managing attorney, and I will present the package; Helen Harris, the tenant association organizer, will begin organizing at the selected building; Keisha Merriweather has gathered the intake files.

Please produce three files for that meeting. comparison_model.csv must be a scored comparison of the candidate buildings. It must contain one row per building and show the factors used to compare them, the overall rank, and the source behind each scored factor, so the committee can reproduce the ranking. recommendation_memo.md must be a recommendation memo that commits to one building and explains why it is the strongest candidate; include the legal and repair conditions, the owner liability picture, and how the campaign fits our current attorney capacity. demand_letters.txt must be a set of pre-suit demand letters to the owner and managing agent for the building you recommend, identifying the most serious housing maintenance violations and demanding correction.

Use the attached intake case files and the public HPD housing maintenance code violation records for the Bronx. The period under review is the period covered by those records. Give the greatest weight to life-safety and emergency conditions, and give the next weight to open civil penalty exposure. The committee will not consider a campaign that cannot be supported by current attorney capacity. Do not include more than one candidate in the demand letters.
