你是皮马县卫生部门的首席生物统计师。我正在编制 2026—2027 学年的户外活动指引；卫生官员和学区负责人将在 10 月规划会议上依据本次分析，决定是否调整全县的户外活动取消阈值及哮喘就诊激增应对计划。现有规划文件假定，学校只有在每日空气质量指数（AQI）达到 150 时才取消户外活动；请在暴露分类中采用该阈值。随附导出数据和 AirNow AQI 技术援助文件已提供给你。

请交付三份工作簿：

  • county_aqi_cancellation_workbook.xlsx：包含清洗后的皮马县每日 AQI 序列、年度 AQI 汇总，以及学校户外活动取消日期清单及各日 AQI。
  • aqi_asthma_model_workbook.xlsx：包含以每日 AQI 和活动取消状态解释每日哮喘急诊就诊次数的主模型，纳入滞后 0—2 天的暴露项，并给出 95% 置信区间。
  • asthma_ed_visit_summary.xlsx：包含每日及年度每万名居民哮喘急诊就诊率，以及结果所依据的假设和局限。

请说明你作出的全部假设，以及会影响结果的数据局限。

英文原文

You are the lead biostatistician for the Pima County Health Department. I am preparing the 2026–2027 school-year outdoor activity guidance, and the health officer and school superintendent will use this analysis at our October planning meeting to decide whether to revise the county's cancellation threshold and asthma surge planning. Our current planning document assumes that school outdoor activities were cancelled only when the daily AQI reached 150; use that threshold in the exposure classification. You have the attached exports and the AirNow AQI technical assistance document. Please deliver three workbooks. county_aqi_cancellation_workbook.xlsx should contain the cleaned daily AQI series for Pima County, the annual AQI summary, and the list of school outdoor activity cancellation days with their daily AQI values. aqi_asthma_model_workbook.xlsx should contain the primary model of daily asthma emergency-department visits as a function of daily AQI and cancellation status, including lagged exposure terms for 0–2 days and 95% confidence intervals. asthma_ed_visit_summary.xlsx should contain daily and annual asthma emergency-department visit rates per 10,000 residents and the assumptions and limitations behind the results.

State any assumptions you make and any limitations of the data that affect the results.
