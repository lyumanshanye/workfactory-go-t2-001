我想请你负责今年专业发展会议预算申请中的差旅部分。你以前做过这类项目的差旅规划，我就简要说明。员工将从各区域办公室前来参会；在材料提交财务部之前，每位被提名人员都必须通过差旅与费用政策审核。规划简报、提名导出记录和其他所需材料均已随附。

申请将提交 Peter Carlson 主持的预算委员会，我必须在 2 月 6 日星期五前报送。会议的交通与住宿费用不得超过 75,000 美元，因此我们申请的这部分金额必须控制在上限以内。

请交付三份文件：

  • conference_travel_budget_FY2026.xlsx：支撑申请的预算模型，列明每位拟参会员工的预计交通与住宿费用及审核结论、其他项目支出，以及申请总额与预算上限的对照。
  • travel_determination_register.csv：每位拟参会员工一行，无论是否获准，均列明预计费用，便于协调员直接导入审批跟踪表。
  • conference_budget_memo.docx：面向委员会的简短备忘录，建议本次申报金额，以适合委员会阅读的详略程度说明哪些人获准、哪些人未获准及原因；说明非差旅项目的计价和处理方式（Peter 的分析员总会追问随行人员与宾客的安排）；列出必须由委员会自行决定的事项。

去年的申请能获批，是因为每笔预算都有我们政策所依据的公开每日津贴标准作支撑，财务部还会抽查这些数字。今年也请采用相同依据。对于登记表中未获准的人员，请提供一段可以转发给其经理的说明。

周四和周五大部分时间我在出差，但可以通过电子邮件联系。

Brenda

英文原文

I'd like you to run the travel piece of our budget application for this year's Professional Development Conference — you've planned the travel side of these programs before, so I'll keep this short. Staff come in from the regional offices, and before anything goes to Finance, every nomination has to clear the travel and expense policy. The planning brief, the nomination exports and the other material you need are attached.

The application goes to Peter Carlson's budget committee, and I have to file it by Friday, February 6. Travel and lodging for the event may not come in above $75,000, so whatever we ask for has to land inside that.

Three deliverables, please:

conference_travel_budget_FY2026.xlsx — the model behind the request: every proposed traveler's estimated travel and lodging cost with the determination for each, the other program items, and the total we'd be asking for against the cap.

travel_determination_register.csv — one row per proposed traveler, cleared or not, with the estimated cost, so my coordinator can load it straight into the clearance tracker.

conference_budget_memo.docx — a short memo recommending the figure we submit, written for the committee: who is cleared, who isn't and why, at the level they read; how the non-travel program items are costed and treated (Peter's analysts always probe the companion and guest arrangements); and anything the committee itself has to decide.

Last year's application cleared because every line stood on the published per diem rates our policy is built on, and Finance spot-checks those figures — keep this estimate on the same footing. Where the register turns someone away, give me the one-paragraph version I can forward to their manager.

I'm travelling most of Thursday and Friday, but email reaches me.

Brenda
